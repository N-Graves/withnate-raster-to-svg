Turn a picture into a real cut file

Trace a PNG or JPEG into an SVG with genuine path nodes — the outlines a Cricut or Silhouette can actually follow, not a bitmap hidden inside an SVG wrapper. It runs on your own machine and nothing is uploaded.

…or drop one here, or paste from the clipboard.

Download the SVG

Two things worth knowing before you start. This traces flat colour, so photographic gradients come out as visible bands — it is at its best on line work, logos and clipart. And a detailed photograph can produce an enormous file, big enough to be slow in whatever you open it in.
